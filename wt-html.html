Your Windscreen

A Critical Safety Component

5 Tips for Keeping your Windscreen in Good Condition

Dirty windscreens can be both a safety hazard and can result in scratching that is difficult to fix. There are some easy steps you can take to prevent this:

1. Replace your windscreen wipers regularly.

2. Keep your windscreen clean.

3. Clean the inside of your windscreen too.

4. Use the correct cleaning materials.

5. Use the correct reservoir additives.

Worn Windscreens Affect your Driving

Studies by the Monash University have shown connections between worn windscreens and road safety. Some of the key findings from this study included: – drivers may take longer to re-adapt their vision following exposure to the stray light effects created through a worn windscreen

– detection distances to objects on the road ahead may be reduced when looking through worn windscreens with stray light effects

Your Windscreen Could Save your Life

Your windscreen provides up to 30% of your car’s structural strength. It also supports the airbag and airbag cover when activated during an accident. A little crack in your glass might seem minor, but can have a significant effect on the safety of your vehicle.

Make sure that you repair a cracked or damaged windscreen as soon as the damage occurs, and always get an expert to repair your windscreen correctly.
